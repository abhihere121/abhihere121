# Go-To-Market Playbook

## Beta motion (first 30 days)

### Week 1: Build proof prototype
- Configure 1 demo store end-to-end
- Generate one sample weekly report with realistic numbers
- Create install walkthrough (3-5 minute Loom)

### Week 2: Recruit 5 design partners
- Channels:
  - Instagram DMs to Shopify fashion founders
  - LinkedIn outreach to D2C operators
  - India D2C communities
- Offer: 30 days free + setup done-for-you

### Week 3: Prove value
- Deliver first weekly WhatsApp report Monday morning
- Run 1 restock broadcast and track conversion
- Collect quantified testimonial:
  - Missed revenue identified
  - Revenue recovered post-restock alert

### Week 4: Publish social proof
- Build landing page with ROI calculator
- Add 2-3 case snippets
- Launch founder-led content posts with data screenshots

## Messaging framework

### Primary message
"You don’t have an inventory problem. You have a visibility problem."

### Proof message
"See exactly which sizes are costing you sales, every week on WhatsApp."

### Offer message
"Install in 5 minutes. Recover missed demand before the next purchase cycle."

## Pricing path
- Free: limited products + email report
- Starter: WhatsApp report
- Growth: customer restock alerts
- Pro: multi-store + automations

## Evidence required before scaling ads
- 10+ stores with 2 weeks of reports
- At least 3 quantified case studies
- Benchmark median missed revenue/store/week
